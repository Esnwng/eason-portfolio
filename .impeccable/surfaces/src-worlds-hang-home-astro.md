# Surface brief: Home (world "hang")

Scope: the whole site rendered by src/worlds/hang/* (Home, WorkIndex, FilmPage, PhotoIndex, GalleryPage, CVPage, NotFound). Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: admissions readers and festival programmers, laptop, under a minute. Job: judge whether Eason Wang is a serious cinematographer and photographer. Proof: Anna's Diary (Chinh Gold 2023, Ethos selection), five photo series. Constraints: content files are the only source of truth; no fabricated claims; privacy (no school/age/city).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one lit exhibition wall. Every work hangs at its own scale with a museum label beneath it, and lighting is the interface: a spot pool says where to look, a label says what you see. It refuses the category arrangement of a full-bleed hero photo with a thumbnail grid under it.

OWN-WORLD: Matte painted wall as the ground of every page, warm graphite (around #2c2d31; never near-black), with spot pools (radial light falloff to ~#4a4a4f) placed where works hang. Prints sit in a thin off-white mat (#f3efe7) with an offset, soft drop shadow. Labels are small card-stock rectangles on one measured scale: title, series, year, medium, count. Wall text and the name are vinyl lettering: wide uppercase grotesk, off-white, at wall scale. One red dot (#c93c2e, ~10px) marks an awarded work. Wayfinding is a small vinyl strip, not a header bar. No gradients-as-decoration, no cards, no eyebrow labels.

STORY: The visitor walks in and the title wall names the practitioner; one print is lit. They walk the hang: a dark screening alcove plays Anna's Diary; rooms of stills follow, each with labels; the award is a vinyl wall text with a red dot; they leave with the CV as a printed handout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third: EASON WANG in vinyl caps stacked two lines at wall scale (clamp 72–140px), tagline in small vinyl caps under it. Right two-thirds: the hero print (site.hero) hung at eye-line inside a soft spot pool, mat and shadow, about 60% of viewport height, its label card beneath. Top-left: a vinyl wayfinding line (Entrance · Film · Photography · CV). The wall continues below into the hang; the next room begins at the fold with the screening alcove doorway. Primary action: the lit print (opens its series) and the screening doorway.

FORM: The gallery hang, position 3 on the ordered grounded list, seed key 23a73f0b. Signature interaction: the spot pools shift subtly with the pointer (the light follows you), and on scroll each room's lights come up (a lights-on reveal driven by the pool's opacity and radius, not a fade-up of elements). Motion grammar: light, never slides.

Raises: one material owns the whole plane (every page and state is the wall); every label is measured from content data; complete keyboard operation with the focus ring as the spot; the CV packs facts into a ruled wall-text panel; the page is paced like a walk, dense rooms then a quiet one.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
