## Direction contract

THESIS: Selection is the cinematographer's craft. The page is the darkroom contact sheet he marked up: strips of frames on black photo paper, the selects circled in grease pencil and pulled forward at full size. It refuses the full-bleed hero with a thumbnail grid.

OWN-WORLD: Black photo paper ground (#0f0f10 with a faint paper texture). 35 mm strips: frames edge to edge with sprocket-hole edges (CSS pattern, not images) and frame numbers along the edge; edge-code lettering in a condensed face for numbers and codes only (it is measurement, not costume). Frame ink is warm white (#f1ede4). Grease pencil orange (#ff5a1f) is the single accent: hand-drawn SVG circles, ticks and arrows with slightly rough strokes; the only words in a marker face are short annotations. Selects sit at 3–4× frame scale, overlapping the strips, with a soft offset shadow. No cards, no eyebrow labels, no gradient text.

STORY: The visitor sees a sheet and understands at once that these are frames he chose. The circled film frame opens the screening. Each strip is one series; pulling a frame forward opens it. The award is an annotation beside the film. The CV is the back of the sheet: a typed index card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Four horizontal strips of real frames fill the viewport edge to edge, sprocket edges top and bottom of each strip, frame numbers along them. The sheet's edge code runs along the top: EASON WANG · CINEMATOGRAPHER & PHOTOGRAPHER, with the nav as further edge-code words (WORK · PHOTOGRAPHY · CV). Two selects are circled in orange and already enlarged, overlapping the strips: the Anna's Diary frame (annotated "screening →" and "Chinh Gold 2023") and the hero still (site.hero). Primary action: the circled film frame.

FORM: The contact sheet, position 1 on the ordered grounded list, seed key 23a73f0b. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a frame lights it as on a light table (a loupe-like brightening and slight lift), and each orange circle draws itself once on first view (stroke-dashoffset). Motion grammar: light table and pencil, nothing slides.

Raises: one material owns the whole plane (every page is the sheet, the CV is its back); every frame number and label comes from content data; complete keyboard traversal frame by frame; the CV packs facts tight on the index card; the sheet reads in strip order, a physical reading order.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
